## Intended Scope (when designed)

Low-poly painterly coastal plants (potted flowers, climbing greenery, shrubs, small trees) with broad silhouettes and painted foliage, readable from the isometric camera. Wind cues may be shader-driven (`docs/concept/08`).

## Before Writing

Requires the island visual direction and a decision on transparency/alpha usage — heavy transparency is discouraged for mobile (`Standards/Materials.md`). Foliage often needs a deliberate alpha vs. solid-geometry choice; resolve and record it.
