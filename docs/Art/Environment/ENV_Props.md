# Environment — Props (STUB)

> **Placeholder. Not a specification.** Small props have not been designed as art assets. Do not treat anything here as settled.

## Intended Scope (when designed)

Small dressing and interactive props (crates, barrels, lanterns, ropes, nets, pots, tools, lifebuoys) as low-poly, atlas-shared, real-time 3D, readable from the isometric camera. The reference art shows several (crates, lantern, potted plants, coiled rope, lifebuoy).

## Before Writing

Requires a prop list tied to gameplay and dressing needs, and a shared-atlas plan grouping props into sets (`Standards/Materials.md`). Interactive props' gameplay authority lives in code, not art (`AGENTS.md`).

## Standards That Will Apply

`ARTSTYLE.md`, `Scale.md`, `Cameras.md`, `Materials.md` (grouped atlases), `Naming.md` (`PROP_*`), `BlenderRules.md` (modularity, collision, pivots).
